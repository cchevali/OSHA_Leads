The structure this family is actually testing

The $1.9M LOI is a diligence frame, not the automatic closing structure. These are the household boundaries and economic levers that decide the final price and capital stack.

Household cash boundary

About $40K stays outside

The family planning case treats roughly $40K of household cash as an acceptable reserve today, contributes $0 of it to closing, and never uses it to solve a business funding or operating gap. Any remaining diligence spend must leave or restore that reserve by closing.

Price follows verified SDE

3.5x reference

Use verified standalone SDE—not the LOI headline—to set the final fixed price. At $510K SDE the indexed reference is about $1.78M; $500K supports about $1.75M, $525K about $1.84M, and $550K about $1.93M.

Seller note

About $200K

The working note is moderate rather than seller-financing the whole deal. It only protects the family if final documents include lender-approved standby, subordination, payment restrictions, and offset rights.

ROBS versus guaranteed debt

$260K current · $400K test

Keep $260K as the current modeled use and make $400K visible as the higher-ROBS planning case. If more equity is needed, test that case before increasing personally guaranteed debt—but only as a joint family decision after CFP, ERISA/ROBS-counsel, and lender review.

Business liquidity

About $200K funded

Working capital is a day-one business use, separate from household cash. Transaction fees, transition compensation, and any winter-retention cost must also be funded before the structure is complete.

Total family failure

Would surprise us

The severe outcome should surprise us—but only if every closing guideline is followed. This is a conditional family judgment, not a measured percentage or a promise. conditional buyer assessment — percentage intentionally withheld

Here, total family failure means the business fails, the ROBS capital committed to it is largely lost, an asset sale leaves a guaranteed debt shortfall, the lender enforces that shortfall, and the household cannot absorb the result without lasting instability.

If any one of those protections is missing, the label resets to “Can't estimate yet”—not low—and the deal reprices, pauses, or stops.

How each major risk is contained

Each risk has a first lever, a fallback, and a point where we stop. These are protections to verify—not claims that the deal is already safe.

Cash-flow headroom

Plan: Price follows verified standalone earnings. At about $510K SDE, the first lever is a lower fixed price—roughly the existing 3.5x guide, about $1.78M—not a larger guaranteed loan.

Fallback: Use a moderate seller note around $200K with lender-approved standby, subordination, payment restrictions, and offset rights. If a gap remains, compare the current $260K ROBS use with an explicit $400K planning case before adding guaranteed debt, only as a joint CFP, ERISA/ROBS-counsel, and lender-approved family decision. More ROBS reduces debt service but increases retirement capital at risk; it is not free protection.

Stop: No closing unless stabilized all-in coverage is at least 1.50x with the final seller-note terms, reserves, and recurring support costs included.
